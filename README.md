# 63 of the Fortune 100 leave AI off their homepage, Amazon and Tesla included

Installmap scored the homepages of the Fortune 100 in September 2026 and checked whether the visible text says "AI" or "artificial intelligence" anywhere. 94 homepages could be scored. 63 of them don't mention it.

The 63 include Amazon (rank 1), Alphabet (5), Berkshire Hathaway (7), JPMorgan Chase (12), Costco (13), Bank of America (20), Ford (22), General Motors (23), Home Depot (25), Target (42) and Tesla (43).

Among the 31 that do: Walmart (its shopping assistant widget introduces itself as an AI assistant), UnitedHealth Group, Apple, Microsoft and Nvidia, which puts "Artificial Intelligence Computing" in its page title.

| Group | Homepages that mention AI |
|---|---|
| Fortune 500 | 29.1% |
| Inc 5000 | 30.3% |
| Unicorns | 53.7% |
| Y Combinator companies | 58.0% |
| YC 2012 batch | 30.4% |
| YC 2025 batches | 78.2% |

We expected the Inc 5000, full of young software firms, to be well ahead of the Fortune 500. The two are level.

Six of the 100 couldn't be scored (Meta's listed domain returned a router page, HCA Healthcare geo-blocked the fetch, and others); the full list and method are in the report.
